FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We’re Here to Help.

Learn how Kodafriq verifies healthcare talent, matches specialized medical professionals with top healthcare facilities, and ensures secure, compliant collaboration.

Kodafriq implements a multi-stage credentialing and verification pipeline: government-issued ID validation, clinical education & professional license authentication directly with official health regulatory boards, verified employment background checks, and objective proctored assessments in medical coding (ICD-10-CM/PCS, CPT, HCPCS), billing, or revenue cycle management.

Upon completing initial registration and identity onboarding, candidates start with a 60% foundation score. As candidates verify credentials, upload certifications, complete technical assessments, and gain client reviews, their verification score dynamically climbs toward 100% verified status.

Our specialized talent pool covers Health Information Management (HIM), Certified Medical Coding (CPC, CCS, CIC, COC), Medical Billing & Claims Adjudication, Revenue Cycle Management (RCM), Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI), and Telehealth Clinical Support.

Because candidates in our talent pool are pre-vetted and credentialed, healthcare facilities and employers typically review candidate profiles, schedule interviews, and finalize digital contracts within 48 to 72 hours, reducing traditional healthcare hiring cycles by over 60%.

All contract funds are held securely in milestone escrow. Payments are released according to agreed milestones or scheduled bi-weekly payouts upon employer sign-off. This ensures total financial transparency, automated audit receipts, and dispute mediation protection for both parties.

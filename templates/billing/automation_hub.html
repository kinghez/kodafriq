Engine 1: Weekly Timesheet Lock
Autonomous weekly billing cycle transition & invoice issuance.
59 23 * * 0
Runs every Sunday at 23:59 UTC/GMT. Evaluates active hourly contracts, locks draft timesheets with hours worked, generates ISSUED employer invoices, dispatches notifications, and automatically pre-populates next week's empty timesheet with 7 daily entries.
Execution Sequence:
  • Finds all DRAFT timesheets closing on/before reference date.
  • Transitions timesheets with hours > 0 into SUBMITTED.
  • Generates ContractInvoice (10% platform fee + pass-through processing).
  • Sends in-app notifications to candidate and employer.
  • Initializes next week's empty timesheet (7 daily entry rows).

Engine 2: Friday Batch Payout
Autonomous non-custodial earnings disbursement to talent.
0 9 * * 5
Runs every Friday at 09:00 UTC/GMT. Evaluates settled employer invoices (payout_status == QUEUED), batches talent earnings, resolves verified Paystack Ghana MoMo or Flutterwave Bank destinations, executes transfers, and updates ledger states.
Execution Sequence:
  • Queries all SETTLED invoices with QUEUED payout status.
  • Groups earnings by candidate and validates verified payout profiles.
  • Executes Paystack Ghana MoMo (GHS) or Flutterwave Bank transfer.
  • Transitions invoice payout status to COMPLETED.
  • Writes immutable PaymentTransaction (PAYOUT) audit log.
